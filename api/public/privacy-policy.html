privacy policy

last updated: july 2026

sentinelpay has created this privacy policy to explain what personal data we collect, when we collect it, why we collect it, and how it is used.

who is sentinelpay

sentinelpay is a crypto risk and compliance company. we provide a monitoring platform that helps crypto businesses screen wallets and transactions, detect exposure to financial crime, and decide whether to accept a payment before funds are released.

sentinelpay is the data controller for personal data processed in connection with our products and services. our full legal entity details, registration number and registered office will be published here before launch. if you have any question about how we handle your data in the meantime, you can reach us at privacy@sentinelpay.org.

what personal data we collect

we may collect, use, store and transfer different kinds of personal data:

identity datafirst name, last name, and, where required to onboard you or verify your identity, identification documents and similar information.
contact dataemail address, company name, telephone number and billing address.
payment databilling details and crypto wallet addresses used to pay for our services.
crypto activity datawallet addresses, transaction identifiers, on-chain history, sanctions and watchlist matches, and other records of activity that help us identify illicit crypto activity and produce a risk score.
technical dataip address, browser type and version, time zone and location, operating system and platform, device identifiers and other technology on the devices you use to access our site and services.
usage datainformation about how you log into, interact with and use our site and services.
marketing datayour preferences in receiving marketing and other communications from us.

how personal data is collected

  • when you visit our website, for example when you complete a form or when we place cookies.
  • when you apply to become a sentinelpay customer or join our newsletter or waitlist.
  • when you interact with us by email, phone or video call. calls may be recorded, and you will be told before any recording starts.
  • when you connect a public key and use the sentinelpay services.
  • when we collect information about prospective customers who may be interested in our services.
  • when we operate our services, for example when we compile sanctions lists or crypto intelligence.

how personal data is stored and protected

we store and process your personal data on cloud infrastructure with reputable providers. we protect it with physical, electronic and procedural safeguards, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and least-privilege access so that only staff who need personal data to do their job can reach it.

how personal data is shared

  • we share crypto activity data and our risk analysis of it with customers as part of the product.
  • we use trusted service providers to whom we may pass personal data under data processing agreements requiring them to protect it to at least our standards.
  • we may share personal data with law enforcement in connection with an investigation to help prevent unlawful activity, or where we are legally obliged to disclose it to a court or regulator.
  • with your permission, we may share event registration details with co-hosts, speakers or sponsors of an event.

we do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for any purpose other than those set out in this policy.

international transfers

we may need to transfer your personal data to countries outside your own, including to service providers located elsewhere. any such transfer will be carried out in compliance with applicable data protection law, using appropriate safeguards.

how we use artificial intelligence

sentinelpay may process personal data using ai, for example to score risk, detect patterns, or support features you have enabled. our use of ai does not constitute solely automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects: a risk score is information you act on, and the decision to accept or reject a payment remains yours. where you enable an ai feature that processes your voice, image or inputs, we will ask for your consent; other ai processing is carried out under legitimate interests.

how long we keep personal data

we keep your personal data only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected for. after that we either anonymise it if we still want it for analytics, pseudonymise it if we believe in good faith we may need it again for a legitimate purpose, or delete it. this does not apply to crypto activity data, which we have a legitimate need to retain to provide our services and maintain the integrity of our risk analysis.

your rights

depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • request access to the personal data we hold about you, and check that we are processing it lawfully.
  • request correction of personal data you believe is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • request deletion of your personal data where we have no legitimate reason to keep processing it.
  • object to processing where we are relying on a legitimate interest.
  • request that we restrict processing, for example while its accuracy is checked.
  • request the transfer of your personal data to another party.
  • withdraw consent where you have given it.

to exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@sentinelpay.org. we may need to confirm your identity first, as a security measure to make sure personal data is not disclosed to anyone who has no right to receive it.

how to contact us

for any privacy question or to exercise your rights, email privacy@sentinelpay.org.

if you have concerns about how we handle your personal data, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection regulator.

sentinelpay's crypto activity data

sentinelpay maintains a database of publicly available cryptocurrency data, such as wallet addresses, transaction ledgers and blockchains. we combine this with information about a wallet's association with known or suspected criminal activity, and with publicly available sanctions lists.

some of this information relates to a person's association with known or suspected criminal activity, which in some jurisdictions is a special category of data. where that is the case, our processing is carried out under a substantial public interest condition, such as the prevention or detection of unlawful acts. sentinelpay is a data controller of the personal data in this database.

customers may submit crypto information to us, such as wallet addresses or transaction hashes. we may combine this with the information in our database to assess the level of risk that the activity is involved with crime or sanctioned parties. we tell the customer that risk level, and the customer decides how to proceed. where our services identify a high risk, a customer may decline a transaction or request further information. in serious cases, information may be disclosed to law enforcement or other agencies working to prevent crime.

cookie notice

our website uses cookies, small text files stored on your device, in two ways:

  • to collect system information such as browser and operating system, the site you arrived from, how long you view pages, the paths you take through the site, and your ip address. this is used for administration and to report aggregate statistics. on its own it does not usually identify a specific individual.
  • to facilitate the login process when registered users access private areas of our site.

most browsers let you delete or disable cookies through their settings. please note that disabling cookies may stop you from accessing private areas of the site.

changes to this policy

we may update this policy from time to time. where changes are significant, we will try to give customers reasonable advance notice, but you should check this page from time to time. the date at the top shows when it was last updated.
